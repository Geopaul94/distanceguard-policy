# Privacy Policy — Distance Guard

Effective and last updated: **11 November 2026**

Distance Guard is published by **oortech** (developer: Geo Paul). This policy covers the Android app **Distance Guard**, application ID `com.ghonest.distanceguard`.

## The short version

- Distance Guard does **not** collect, transmit, or sell personal data.
- Camera frames are analysed on your phone in real time and are never saved or uploaded.
- The app has **no internet permission**, so it cannot send data anywhere.
- No accounts, ads, analytics, or crash-reporting services.

## Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not ask you to create an account and contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting software.

## Camera and face data

The front camera is used only while protection is running, to estimate how far the phone is from your face.

- Frames are processed live and entirely on your device using Google ML Kit face detection. The model is bundled in the app and works offline.
- The only value produced is a numeric eye-spacing measurement used to estimate distance. It is used for the warning and lock decision and then discarded.
- No photos, videos, or camera frames are stored, saved to the gallery, or shared.
- Camera data is never sent to us, to Google, or to anyone else. The app does not request internet access.
- Detection stops when you stop protection or turn the screen off.

## Information stored on your device

The app saves these settings in its private storage:

- the calibration value (eye-spacing reference from the 40 cm setup),
- warning threshold, close-use delay, and whether warn-then-lock is enabled.

These values stay on your phone, are not readable by other apps, and are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. They are never transmitted.

## Permissions and why they are needed

| Permission | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Camera | Measure the distance to your face while protection is on. |
| Display over other apps | Show the warning card above whatever app you are using. |
| Notifications | Show the required foreground-service notification and quick controls. |
| Device administrator (screen lock only) | Lock the screen after the warning countdown if warn-then-lock is enabled. The app cannot erase data, change your password, or make other policy changes. You can revoke this at any time in Android settings. |
| Foreground service / camera | Keep the protection session reliable while you use other apps. |

The app does **not** request access to location, contacts, microphone, photos or files, SMS, call logs, or the internet.

## Sharing and selling data

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share personal data, because we do not collect any. The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

## Children's privacy

Distance Guard is a general-audience utility and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Security

Because the app does not transmit data, there is no server, database, or account that could be breached. Your settings remain in the app's private storage on your phone.

## Your choices

- Stop protection at any time from the app, its notification, or the warning card.
- Revoke camera, display-over-other-apps, notification, or device-admin access in Android Settings.
- Clear the app's data or uninstall it to remove all saved settings.

## Accuracy and health disclaimer

Distance Guard estimates distance from camera measurements. Estimates can be affected by lighting, glasses, face angle, and camera hardware, so treat them as approximate. The app is a habit aid, not a medical device, and does not provide medical advice or diagnosis.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
